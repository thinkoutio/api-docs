Everything your company's money has done is already in ThinkOut — every
transaction in and out, every account, every counterparty, sorted into
categories and labels that mean something to your business.

Now you can ask about it in plain language. Not just what the numbers are, but
what sits behind them: which clients are slowing down, where a cost crept up,
whether the month is going the way you think it is. The kind of answer that
makes a decision feel less like a guess.

**If you want to ask questions in plain language**, connect ThinkOut to an AI
assistant. You sign in with your ThinkOut account, no keys involved, and from
there you just ask.

**If you want the data inside your own systems**, use the REST API. An API key
bound to one workspace, and from there your BI tool, your ERP, the script that
runs every Monday morning — whatever you already have.

## The same numbers, either way

Both routes are read-only. Nothing can be edited, categorised or paid for from
here — this is a window onto your data, not a set of controls.

And both return figures computed by ThinkOut, using the same rules as the cash
flow screen: transfers between your own accounts excluded, split payments
counted once, foreign currency converted at the rate on the movement date.

So a total you see in a conversation, the same total in your dashboard, and the
same total in the app are one number, not three that happen to be close. That
consistency is the point. It is what makes the data safe to build on.

## Where to start

If you are setting this up for yourself, start with the
[MCP server](/guides/mcp) — it takes a few minutes and needs nothing technical.

If you are building something, start with
[authentication](/guides/authentication), then the
[data model](/guides/data-model), which explains how accounts, movements,
categories and labels fit together before you write your first request.

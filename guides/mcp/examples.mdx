## Your first question to the assistant

Once the connector is live, you can start with a question like:

> What is my balance right now, and how did it move over the last six months?

You should get a table with the figures, the period and the currency. If you
have more than one workspace, mention in the question which one you want the
answer for. If you don't, the assistant asks, and then remembers for the rest of
the conversation.

## What you can ask

Questions can be written exactly the way you would say them out loud: "total
inflows from the first of April until today", "total results for last semester",
"results for this September against last September". Don't worry about
mistyping a word — the assistant handles typos and missing diacritics.

<Note>
  Questions marked with a <strong>*</strong> are answered by the assistant
  reasoning over ThinkOut's totals. The assistant tells you which period and
  which assumptions it used, and you can read them as a documented
  interpretation, not as a figure ThinkOut computed. When a what-if question
  matches a scenario you saved in ThinkOut, the assistant reads that
  scenario's figures instead and names it.
</Note>

## What you can ask it to change

The assistant can also tidy up your data: categorise transactions and
forecasts, set counterparties and labels, pair transfers, match payments to
forecasts, write categorisation rules and set budgets. Ask for it the way you
would ask a colleague. You can name the items, or describe them with the same
filters you use in a question, such as "the Lidl card payments since June".

A change to one item, where nothing is lost, happens as soon as you approve
the assistant's own prompt. When a change touches several items, or replaces
or removes something, the assistant first tells you in words what would
change and waits for your yes. After the change it reports what each item held
before, so you can ask it to put things back. [Changes](/guides/mcp/changes)
lists everything it can change and what stays in ThinkOut.

<Note>
  Changes need a connection that can change data and the Editor role in the
  workspace. A connection made before changes were available is read only
  until you connect again. See [Who can change data](/guides/mcp/changes#who-can-change-data).
</Note>

## What a change looks like

For a change to several items, the assistant shows the list before it touches
anything:

> **You:** Put every Orange payment from this year under Telecom.
>
> **Assistant:** 14 transactions would move to Telecom: 11 are uncategorised and
> 3 are under Utilities. Shall I go ahead?

Nothing changes until you answer. When you agree, the assistant makes the
change and says what it did. If nothing would change, for example because the
transactions are already under Telecom, it tells you so at once.

## Filtering and grouping

You can narrow a question by as many criteria as you like at once, just as on
the cash flow screen: category, label, bank, account, counterparty. The same
criteria pick the items for a change, as in "label the Banca Transilvania card
payments since June".

Grouping works by day, week or month, optionally with one further dimension:
counterparty, category, label, account or bank. When you do not add a second
dimension, each period also shows its starting balance, transfers, excluded
movements and ending balance.

A cash flow question keeps what has moved and what is still planned apart, and
the two add up to the screen. Forecasts have no account, so a bank or account
filter narrows the actual figures and the balances; the planned part covers
the whole workspace.

---

Connect your ThinkOut account to an AI assistant and ask it about your company's
finances the way you would ask the colleague who does the analysis. The
assistant reads your profile of balances, transactions, counterparties,
categories and labels, and answers with figures computed by ThinkOut.

The connection is read-only: you can't change anything in your account from a
conversation.

<Note>
  One connection covers every workspace you have in ThinkOut. If you only have
  one, the assistant goes straight to your figures. If you have several, tell it
  which one you want to work on — if you don't, it will ask, and it remembers
  your answer for the rest of the conversation.
</Note>

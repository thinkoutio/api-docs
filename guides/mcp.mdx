Connect your ThinkOut account to an AI assistant and ask it about your company's
finances the way you would ask the colleague who does the analysis. The
assistant reads your balances, transactions, forecasts, budgets, scenarios,
counterparties, categories and labels, and answers with figures computed by ThinkOut, for what
has happened and for what you have planned.

The assistant can also tidy up your data when you ask: categorise
transactions and forecasts, match payments to forecasts, write categorisation rules and set
budgets. It changes only what already exists, shows you a change to several
items, or one that replaces or removes something, before it makes it. It
never creates or deletes transactions or bank accounts.

<Note>
  One connection covers every workspace you have in ThinkOut. If you only have
  one, the assistant goes straight to your figures. If you have several, tell it
  which one you want to work on — if you don't, it will ask, and it remembers
  your answer for the rest of the conversation.
</Note>
